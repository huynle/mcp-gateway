Before you write

  • An agent cannot connect: sign in to the portal and check the agent on the Harnesses or Tokens page; sign it in again if its access was revoked or expired.
  • An app says "reconnect": open Connections and connect the account again.
  • A tool call waits: it may need your approval on the Approvals page.
  • Something you did not expect happened: your Activity page lists every call made in your name; revoke the agent and tell us.
